Privacy Policy for Twitter Classic

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Twitter Classic is a browser extension that changes the appearance of x.com. It does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

What the extension stores

It saves your feature settings (which switches are on or off) using Chrome's local extension storage on your own device. This data never leaves your browser and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

What the extension reads

It runs only on x.com pages so it can change the logo, a fixed list of interface labels, colors and the font. It does not read, record or send your posts, messages, account details or browsing activity.

Network and third parties

The extension makes no network requests, loads no remote code, uses no analytics and shares nothing with third parties.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new date.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue on GitHub.
